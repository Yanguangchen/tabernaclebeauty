The ultimate guide to aftercare for eyelash extensions

24 February 2026 · Treatments

To keep your lashes looking full and fluttery, avoid water and steam for the first 24 hours. Gently brush them daily with a clean spoolie to prevent tangling.

Steer clear of oil-based makeup removers, as they can break down the lash adhesive. Stick to oil-free cleansers and treat your extensions with care to maximize their lifespan.

Why regular body waxing is better than shaving

18 February 2026 · Beauty Tips

Waxing removes hair from the root, meaning it takes much longer to grow back compared to shaving. Over time, regular waxing can also make your hair grow back finer and softer.

Plus, it acts as a gentle exfoliation, removing dead skin cells and leaving your skin feeling incredibly smooth and refreshed for weeks.
