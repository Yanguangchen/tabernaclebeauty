Body waxing in Tampines: a first visit

The waxing menu publishes a price for each area, and several lines have a different figure for men. This guide is the list to read before you message Jessyca, so the booking matches the area you actually want.

Prices on the waxing menu

These are the published prices in SGD. Where a men’s price is printed, it is shown beside the first figure.

  • Upper lip $10, men $12. Upper lip and chin $15, men $20.
  • Eyebrow $12, men $15. Full face $35, men $40.
  • Underarm $20, men $25.
  • Half hand $28, men $33. Full hand $35, men $40.
  • Half legs $35, men $40. Full legs $45, men $50.
  • Full arm and full leg $80, men $90.
  • Full back $35, men $40. Stomach $30, men $35.
  • Bikini $28, ladies only. Brazilian $55, ladies only. Full body $180, ladies only.

The full table is on the waxing price list. Brow threading is a different menu and starts from $9. The threading guide covers that visit. A short note on waxing and shaving is already on the blog. This page is the first-visit price map.

What to put in the WhatsApp

Jessyca’s WhatsApp is +65 8457 4640. Name the area, say if the booking is for a man where the menu has a second price, and say if you want more than one area. Bikini, Brazilian, and full body are listed as ladies only. An appointment is the better plan for a larger area so the visit is not squeezed behind a brow walk-in.

The studio is in Tampines. Come with the skin intact. If the area is sunburnt, broken, or you have used a strong retinoid there, say so before you travel. This page does not promise how long the result lasts.
