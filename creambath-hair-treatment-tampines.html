What a creambath visit is for in Tampines

Creambath at Tabernacle Beauty is deep-conditioning care with a scalp massage, for hair that needs shine, softness, and movement. It is not a facial, and it is not a colour appointment.

What the service card actually promises

The home page describes creambath as deep-conditioning care with scalp massage that restores shine, softness, and healthy movement. That is the whole public description. It does not publish a duration, a product line, or a fixed price. The studio’s own citation note says creambath is quoted, not given a public fee. Message Jessyca on WhatsApp at +65 8457 4640 and ask for the current quote before you hold a time.

When it is the wrong booking

If the hair needs a new colour, book colour, not a creambath standing in for dye. If the brows are the appointment, the threading guide and the embroidery aftercare guide are the pages that match. A facial is chosen from the facial menu, from a classic facial at $60 up to LED at $170, and that menu is for skin. Creambath does not replace any of those.

What to say when you message

Say whether the hair is coloured, recently permed, or simply dry from the weather, and whether you want the creambath on its own. The studio is in Tampines. A street number and a fixed weekly timetable are not published. Appointments are recommended. Walk-ins are the note attached to eyebrow threading, not to hair services.
